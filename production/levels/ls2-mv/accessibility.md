# Accessibility review: LS2-mv (terrain movement B0/B1 + 12 set pieces), phone readability

Date: 2026-11-07. Reviewer: accessibility-specialist. Target: WCAG 2.1 AA as the default, plus design/content-expansion.md §9
(telegraph >= 36 f aimed / >= 44 f area, >= 3 game px thick; rear spawns warned).

Method and limits: static read of `src/terrainb.ts`, `src/render/moveprops.ts`, `src/render/hud.ts`, `src/render/biomeart.ts`,
`src/enemies/spawn.ts`, `src/biomes.ts`, plus the contact sheets (s0 ice/acid/fire/snot, sets3). Spike widths were computed
from the profile formulas. NOT ASSESSED (no phone/Fold build, no colour-vision simulation run, no capture of the moving
frames): true on-device size and thumb occlusion, and a CVD filter pass. Colour findings below are by luminance reasoning on
the stills, not a simulation. Labels: BLOCKING / RECOMMENDED / NICE TO HAVE.

Scale reference: 480 logical px on a ~6 in phone (~2.4 in/…): 1 logical px is roughly 0.3 mm on a 14 cm-wide screen, so a 1 px
feature is a hairline and 3 px is about 1 mm. On the Fold cover screen the picture is smaller still.

## Findings

## BLOCKING items

1. Fire B0 spire tips collide at under 3 px (`src/terrainb.ts:70`).
2. Ice B0 tooth tips collide at under 3 px (`src/terrainb.ts:57`).
3. Chimney/geyser/jet warning is a 1 px dashed outline, under the 3 px rule, with the vent mouth under the HUD strip (`src/render/biomeart.ts:652,666-667`, `src/terrainb.ts:28`).

## Suggested follow-ups

- Terrain edges: add a unit test in `tests/unit/terrainb.test.ts` asserting for every feature that the collision width at its apex (a 3 px run within 1 px of the minimum edge) is >= 3 px at `every` and `span` extremes.
- Run `tools/movement-shots.mjs` once more after the clamp and keep new shots in `production/qa/evidence/ls2-mv/move/`; add a CVD-filtered capture (CSS `filter` matrices for deuteranopia/protanopia) of fire B0/B1 and snot B0/B1, because the CVD and on-device size checks were NOT ASSESSED here.
- A Fold cover-screen shot for the four B0 bands (thumb-occlusion mock: grey 140x140 px at the left-bottom corner).
