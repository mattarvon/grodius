All five kill folders and carn5 are empty today. They stay silent and show no window until Matt records them in
the booth; the banner word still shows as before.

**Doubling up, and why.** A kill word **queues**: it is rare and it is the payoff of a fight, so it shouldn't be
lost to a hype line. OPENED is **skipped**: a 5-chain comes round every few seconds in a busy stretch, and a queued
OPENED would land long after the chain it named. A skipped OPENED does not start its 20 s cooldown.

## Open risks and follow-ups

- **Queued lines can outlive the moment** (web-specialist #6). A delayed `start()` is already scheduled on the
  audio clock, so a line queued behind a long one still plays after a death or `newGame`, with no window off the
  HUD. It is bounded by clip length (at most one line plus .25 s). Stopping live voice lines on game over or a new
  game is a separate decision.
- **Voice lines now pass the cap,** including hype lines and the corpse plea, which previously could be swallowed
  on a full frame. There are only a handful of voice lines, and they were already exempt from eviction.
- **`tools/booth/slots.ts` marks `kill-*` and `carn5` as `wired: false`.** Booth isn't on main yet. Whichever lane
  merges second should flip those to `wired: true`.
