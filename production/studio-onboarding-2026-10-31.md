## Director notes

### Creative director — vision risks
- Pillar 1 vs the power accounting (Design, Programming): the ship's art tier counts contraptions, but difficulty only counts them from Descent 2 through over() (src/render/ship.ts vs src/step.ts:25, src/depth.ts:29-30). perkPower() counts 8 of 24 perks (src/perks.ts:255-257). So how monstrous the ship looks and how hard the game gets disagree, which undermines 'you become what you kill'.
- Pillar 3 vs the recent balance commits (Design, QA): 48eb7cb/d0115f4 moved difficulty from enemy HP to fire and density, but the Gravity Drive still scales HP with rank (src/boss/update.ts:21-22), Descent 3 fodder carries 4.7-7.6x HP from depth (spawn.ts:151, depth.ts:32), and flat-damage perks (SNOT ROCKET, SPORE COAT) lose value. Together these push toward sponges and away from Gradius-style pressure, and nobody has decided which philosophy is canon.
- Pillar 3 vs the economy (Economy): stages pay double in every run from Descent 2 on, so deepPay is not exclusive to DESCEND DEEPER (src/stages.ts:28). An escaped boss still pays biomass and still breaks seals, contrary to README.md:93. An S grade is worth only about 140 more biomass per descent than a C, so skill barely changes progression. A biomass sink could easily turn into pay-for-power unless it is held to 'options, not power'.
- Pillar 2 is violated in the shipped build (Art, QA): postFX draws the hell image, the washes and glitch strips over enemy bullets (src/render/screens.ts:470-474, hell.ts:66-87), and glitch can draw a bullet up to 14 px from its hitbox. - Pillar 4 vs content reuse (Level design): the acid and snot biome bosses are both maw(true) with one shared update case (src/biomes.ts:45,68; enemies/update.ts:509-558), so a route can meet the Maw pattern two or three times in one descent. openFork can also fail to offer an unvisited biome (biomes.ts:224-226).
- Pillar 4 vs untested late descents (Programming, QA): d0115f4 raised density and fire rate by up to about 2x with no re-measurement. The Nail Choir's nailbars never fire at pow 2 (update.ts:396-397, spawn.ts:369), and drone fire scales with loop twice (update.ts:51 vs depth.ts:48). 'Hard' turning into 'broken' or 'unreadable' in Descent 3+ is likely and unmeasured.
- Pillar 5 vs tone drift (Narrative, Localization): 'UNCRUCIFIED' (enemies/update.ts:738), the 'CROSSES' kill source (mutations.ts:51), HALO RIPPLE crosses (render/shipparts.ts:391) and the 'crucify' set (biomes.ts:38,173) survive the no-crosses pass. The hype man is a signature comedy beat, and right now it often misfires or goes silent.
- Comfort and safety vs the gross-out identity (UX, QA, Art): Flashes defaults to ON (save.ts:6), and OFF still leaves a 0.4 white wash, an ungated red wash, glitch tearing and corridor flicker (hell.ts:80-86, fx/update.ts:316-317). ## Design (game-designer, leading systems-designer, level-designer, economy-designer, prototyper)

I spot-checked the specialists' main claims against the code, and nearly all of them hold. The design is in good shape: the four progression layers (perks, kill-count grafts, Infirmary contraptions, depth scaling), the biome fork structure and the probe-driven tuning are all solid.

The real problem is drift. The last two balance commits, 48eb7cb (rank no longer raises enemy HP) and d0115f4 (pressure uncapped, pow cap now 2.0), changed the game's rule: power is meant to show up as more fire and more enemies, not as tougher enemies. Several parts of the code and every design doc were never updated to match:
- The Gravity Drive's HP still scales with rank, and the code comment explaining why is now false (src/boss/update.ts:21-22).
- perkPower() counts only 8 of the 24 perks, and its comment still says "NOT wired yet" even though step.ts:26 uses it (src/perks.ts:255-257).
- formation() now adds up to 6 extra drones (src/enemies/spawn.ts:215), which can merge the acid stage's two-wall set piece into one.
- Some damage sources are flat numbers that ignore the damage multipliers: SNOT ROCKET dmg 4 (src/player.ts:322) and SPORE COAT 1.2 (src/mutations.ts:233). Meanwhile Descent 3 fodder has about 4.7-7.6x HP: (1+.4L) from spawn.ts:151 times f() from depth.ts:32.
- docs/pacing.md was last changed in dfa0c30, before both commits, and README.md:91-93 still describes the old stage layout.

The economy has three real contradictions:
- Stages pay double in every run from Descent 2 on, not only in DESCEND DEEPER runs. deepPay() checks only the loop number (src/stages.ts:28).
- When a stage boss escapes, the stage is graded C but still pays biomass and still counts toward breaking seals (stages.ts:63-69). README.md:93 says an escape pays no reward.
- Nothing is left to spend biomass on once the Infirmary is bought out, around Descent 5-6.

On content, the acid and snot biomes both use the Maw as their boss (src/biomes.ts:45,68), the same boss that ends Open Orbit. A route through either of them meets the same attack pattern twice or more in one descent.

There is no design/gdd/ yet, so none of these formulas has a single written source, and none has a unit test.

My recommended order:
1. Settle the power rule and write it down.
2. Ship a bundle of small fixes.
3. Re-run the probes and update pacing.md.
4. Then content: biome-specific Maws and a biomass sink to soak up post-buyout savings.
Build the cheap prototyping tools alongside, so each of these changes can be tried in a few seconds instead of minutes of play.

### Risks
- Tuning against the stale pacing.md numbers will push balance the wrong way. All the boss and midboss TTK tables were measured while rank still raised enemy HP.
- The power rule touches difficulty, ship art tier and gun FX. Changing the weights in step.ts alone will make what the ship looks like disagree with how hard the game gets. The ship art already counts contraptions and difficulty doesn't (src/render/ship.ts vs src/step.ts:25).

- The new CCGS framework's standards (external config files for gameplay values, a prototypes/ folder that may not import production code, engine-reference docs, src/ treated as Godot-only) don't fit this codebase. If they are applied unconfigured, they will block small fixes or push churn into the docs. Configure project.yaml and the /prototype rules before using its gates.
- Writing the GDDs before the power rule is decided would just write down the current inconsistencies as if they were intended.

#### systems-designer

Grodius runs four stacked progression layers, each with its own currency, all hand-coded as TypeScript tables:
(1) **Per-run perks** (src/perks.ts:14-41). There are 24 weighted mutations: 14 stat perks, 6 build-definers including the GLASS JAW and TAPEWORM trade-offs, and 4 biome-locked perks. They are bought with XP on an `xpNeed` curve that turns quadratic after level 10 (perks.ts:46), plus a lump of XP per stage (perks.ts:48). Perks survive death.
(2) **Kill-count grafts** (src/mutations.ts GM table, lines 11-110). Killing a set number of a given enemy kind grows a capsule. Gun grafts are limited by `gunSlots()` and older ones are sloughed when you go over. Every graft loses one level on death (flow.ts:165-176).
(3) **Permanent contraptions** (src/contraptions.ts RIG, lines 27-50). Bought with biomass in the Infirmary, with 3 levels each and 1-3 sockets.
(4) **Depth scaling** (src/depth.ts). From Descent 2 on, enemy HP, fire cadence and burst size go up based on `loop` and on `over()`, which is uncapped power past the rank cap.

Power is fed back into difficulty through `G.pow`/`G.rank` (step.ts:25-27), which enemy HP, wave density and boss cooldowns read (spawn.ts:177,215,369; boss/update.ts:22). src/shipform.ts is presentation only: it compares the per-frame form descriptor between frames to animate parts sprouting and withering. src/state.ts is a 2-line shared mutable `$` singleton.

Tuning is evidence-driven through Playwright probes (perk-pace, pod-probe, rig-probe, dps-probe, playtest), and docs/pacing.md §3.7/§3.9 records the measured targets (LV3 after stage 1, LV10-11 at the end of Descent 1). There is no design/gdd/ content (design/ only holds CLAUDE.md and registry). There are no unit tests for any formula: tools/harness.mjs is a browser regression run and does not test xpNeed, critMul, depthHP or over().

Bosses and midbosses: 5 midbosses (Saucer, Nail Choir, Dissolving Womb, Butcher's Apprentice, Brood Mother). Each leaves at no cost after ~23 s and shows a HUD fuse. #### economy-designer

Grodius has one persistent currency, biomass ($.meta.bio, saved under localStorage `grodius.v1` in src/save.ts:62-73), and one in-run currency, pod fragments (NEED table, src/player.ts:22-30). Biomass comes from four places: kill orbs, which are physical pickups that expire after 600 frames or when they scroll off (src/enemies/update.ts:697-706, collected at src/fx/update.ts:184-208); a stage-grade payout of S40/A25/B12/C5, doubled from Descent 2 by deepPay() (src/stages.ts:20-28, 63-66); +30 per mini-boss pick, also doubled by deepPay (src/enemies/update.ts:864-865); and +5 for each pod picked up for a maxed slot (src/player.ts:52-53). Multipliers stack. HARVEST GLAND gives +25% per level at spawn, BLOOD MONEY gives +30% per level at pickup (src/fx/update.ts:200), and elites pay x2.5 (src/enemies/elite.ts:16,45). Biomass is credited to meta live as you pick it up, so dying or abandoning keeps everything.

There are three sinks, all in the Infirmary (src/menus.ts:123-260). There are 7 contraptions with a buy price plus 2 level-ups each (src/contraptions.ts:28-34), 2 sockets at 450/1000 (contraptions.ts:38), and 6 UPG grafts (src/save.ts:76-112). The first contraption is free (menus.ts:133). docs/pacing.md §3.7 and §3.9 hold the calibration: about 1.1-1.2k / 1.7-1.9k / 2-3k biomass per descent, a full rack around 9.4k, the whole Infirmary around 13.5k, and full buyout around Descent 5-6. Within a run, rank is G.pow = weapon/graft/perk power / 14, capped at 1 (src/step.ts:24-27). From Descent 2 on, depth scaling (src/depth.ts) adds bolted contraption levels at 0.035 each. Two probes cover this: tools/pod-probe.mjs measures biomass per descent, and tools/shop-probe.mjs only checks the shop UI flows. tools/progression.mjs only logs loadout and power, not biomass. The new studio design/ folder has no economy document yet. pacing.md is the de facto economy spec.

#### prototyper

Grodius is very cheap to prototype in at the code level, and very expensive to prototype in at the feel level. The code side: there is no engine and no build step to wait on. Shared mutable state is a single untyped bag (`export const $: any = {}`, src/state.ts:2), 52 of 78 src files are `// @ts-nocheck`, and `strict` is false (tsconfig.json:8). A new enemy is a `mk()` call (src/enemies/spawn.ts:149), a `case` in the `updEnemy` switch (src/enemies/update.ts:27), a draw branch in src/render/enemies.ts and one entry in the biome `SPAWN` map (src/biomes.ts:153-160). The last new-enemy batch (31a2eb8: slime, mutant, sentry) took 4 source files and about 290 lines of logic. Stage pacing is data-ish: `at(x, fn)` scripts with remix variants (src/enemies/scripts.ts:27-60) and biome mix/remix/SET tables (src/biomes.ts:38-72, 163-172). These are the places to try a new wave or set-piece idea in minutes.

The verification side is real but only partly open to people. The game runs at a fixed 60 Hz `step()` (src/main.ts:43-58, src/step.ts:66). Tools take over Math.random, performance.now and rAF and drive the live Vite modules through `await import('/src/...')`. That gives a seeded, headless sandbox, and 36 tools use it (balance probes, screenshot rigs, a playtest bot in tools/playtest.mjs). The flaw for prototyping is that this sandbox is only reachable through Playwright scripts. Each script rebuilds the same setup boilerplate, and a human can't use it. A person who wants to feel a new boss pattern or mechanic in the actual game still has to play from Open Orbit to the right place. ## Programming (lead-programmer)

The Programming department's assessment is that the Grodius codebase is in good shape. Its custom engine is well built: a fixed 60 Hz step (src/main.ts:42-59), hard-capped particle pools, idle-time prebuilds and a 480x270 world under hi-res eyes. #### ai-programmer

Enemy and boss AI is hand-written, per-frame imperative code over plain mutable objects. There is no behaviour-tree or FSM framework. src/enemies/update.ts (1016 lines) is one `updEnemy` switch on `e.k` that covers about 20 kinds: drone flight patterns, corpse, eye, crawler, hatch, womb, cross, hook, flayer, nailbar, the Crucifer and Butcher stage bosses, the four biome midbosses (choir, cyst, prentice, brood), the Maw, and `updNew` for slime, mutant and sentry. Each "smart" enemy is an ad-hoc string state machine using `e.st`/`e.ws`, `e.wt` (frames in the current state), `e.cool` and `e.att` (an attack counter that picks the next attack with `att % n`). Targeting is almost entirely `aimA()`, an instant atan2 to `$.P` (spawn.ts:191). Telegraphs track the player and then lock (`wrapA` lerp). Bullet patterns are built from three BulletML-lite primitives: `fan`, `ring` and `every` (spawn.ts:363-369). src/enemies/ufo.ts holds the Open Orbit Saucer midboss (lash / volley / lash / brood cycle). src/enemies/elite.ts is an overlay applied after `mk()`. src/enemies/scripts.ts holds the per-descent remixed spawn scripts. src/boss/update.ts holds the Gravity Drive final boss: armour plates, then an eye phase, then an angry phase, with a tracked-then-locked laser, aimed fans, a spiral in phase 2, corpse spawns and tentacle contact.
Difficulty comes from four multipliers spread across files. `BS()` sets bullet speed from loop and pow. `every()` shortens attack gaps with pow. `cdm()`/`firem()`/`xtra()` add depth scaling (src/depth.ts). The midboss cooldown formula adds `(1-.3*rank)`. The two commits from 2026-10-30 (48eb7cb and d0115f4) moved pressure from HP to fire rate and density and raised pow's cap to 2.0. Those changes did not fully reach every consumer, and most of the issues below come from that. All six domain files are `// @ts-nocheck`. The project has no `tests/` directory. Validation relies on tools/harness.mjs, which monkey-patches `Math.random`, and on the *-probe.mjs bots.

#### performance-analyst

Grodius renders every frame with Canvas2D. The playfield is drawn on a 480x270 offscreen canvas (core.ts:16-21). present()/flushLo() (render/eyes.ts:473-504) scales that canvas up to a display canvas $.DK times larger, capped at 4x (input.ts:194-225), and the photo eyes are then drawn on top at full resolution from EYEQ. The loop (main.ts:41-56) runs fixed 60 Hz steps, at most 5 per rAF, and renders once. autoQuality (main.ts:58-69) lowers DK by one step, down to a floor of 2x, when 120 frames average more than 19.2 ms. Every particle list has a hard cap and is removed from with swap-and-pop: drops 4800, gibs 720, mists 340, sparks 500, flashes 140, decals 1800, eshots 260 (fx/spawn.ts, enemies/spawn.ts:179). Explosion and hit sprites come from fixed pools (fx/fxsprites.ts:13,73). Boss art is pre-built in idle slots after boot (main.ts:70-77).

Measurement tools: src/perf.ts is a ?perf overlay that times step, world, fx, hud and present per frame, and window.__perf() returns the numbers. tools/perf-probe.mjs drives 10 heavy scenes headless in desktop Chromium with 4x CPU throttling, with an optional profiler and an ELITE crowd switch. The 2026-10-25 perf pass (CHANGELOG.md:80-88) found that GPU and compositor work dominated, and it was measured on the dev rig's integrated GPU, not on a phone. The newest measurements (docs/pacing.md:302-306) already show render p95 of 16.7-23 ms at 4x throttle in biome fights. The next commit, d0115f4, then raised spawn density and fire rate and was never re-measured. I found no frame budget written down anywhere (project.yaml, docs/, design/) and no record of any on-device Android profile.

#### security-engineer

Grodius is an offline, single-player game with no backend, accounts, leaderboard, analytics or network calls. So the real security surface is small: (1) one localStorage save blob, `grodius.v1`, written by src/save.ts and shared by the web (GitHub Pages) and Android (Capacitor WebView) builds; (2) the Android shell: capacitor.config.ts, AndroidManifest.xml (INTERNET + VIBRATE only), and a FileProvider left over from the Capacitor template; (3) the GitHub Actions pipeline, which optionally signs APKs with a keystore taken from repo secrets, publishes a public rolling `latest` prerelease and a public `ci-log` release on failure, and force-pushes to gh-pages; (4) a large set of third-party CC0 / CC BY / OGA-BY assets, recorded in CREDITS.md and credited on the title screen (src/render/screens.ts:131-133).

save.ts loads with a try/catch, validates and deduplicates the contraption arrays, clamps `cl` and `socket`, and includes a v1->v2 migration that refunds biomass. It saves often: every menu action, at stage clears (stages.ts:99), every 600 ticks (step.ts:61), on `pagehide` (input.ts:29) and on Capacitor `pause` (main.ts:107-108). The file is `@ts-nocheck` (save.ts:1). Nothing protects the save against corruption beyond that one catch, there is no schema version on the top-level key, and there is no backup or export. Cheat resistance doesn't matter here because nothing is shared. The things worth protecting are the player's progress, the signing key, and licence compliance.

## Art (art-director, with technical-artist and ux-designer)

Grodius has a strong, deliberate look: crisp 480x270 pixel art, high-resolution photo eyes drawn on top, real JWST/RODA imagery for skies and tissue, and an over-the-top gross-out layer built into every boss. What it lacks is any written visual standard. design/ holds only CLAUDE.md and registry/entities.yaml, and the only written visual rule is a code comment, the lime enemy-fire rule at src/render/hud.ts:237-305. The art now comes from three sources: code-drawn vector work (enemies.ts, shipparts.ts, the gross/* bosses), around 20 OpenGameArt picks by several different artists repainted toward the palette (CHANGELOG.md:30), and science photos. Without an art bible, every new biome, boss or agent-built pass risks drifting in style and in readability.

The most important problems for the department are about readability and safety, not polish. postFX() runs after drawEShots() (src/render/screens.ts:470-474), so the hell image (.92 alpha, sometimes 'difference' blend, jittered by up to 4 px), the red and white washes, and the glitch strip copies (shifted up to 14 px) all cover or move enemy bullets. That breaks the game's one readability rule. Turning HELL FLASHES OFF still leaves a 0.4 white wash (hell.ts:85) and an ungated red wash (hell.ts:80-82), and glitch still fires with flashes off (fx/update.ts:316).

I checked the specialists' key claims. Most hold. The technical-artist's high-severity RNG issue is real code hygiene but overstated: R is plain Math.random (src/core.ts:33), and the shipping game has no seed. Seeding happens only in tools, which override Math.random (tools/harness.mjs:8). So the damage is to the determinism of probes and playtests, not to players.

Gore intensity is set by six separate hardcoded GROSS=1 constants (textures.ts:21, gross/maw.ts:17, midbosses.ts:17, ufo.ts:25, drive.ts:20, biomebosses.ts:18). Players have no single dial for it, and neither does the art department. The raw asset pipeline cannot be reproduced on this laptop because assets-raw/ is git-ignored and missing here.

### Risks
- The asset pipeline cannot be rebuilt from the repo. assets-raw/ is git-ignored and missing on this laptop clone, there is no requirements file, and sprite_prep.save() deletes the old strips before writing (sprite_prep.py:127-129). A re-prep from the wrong machine could silently wipe shipped sprites. Run any art regeneration from the Windows box (E:\projects\grodius) until a manifest exists.
- Licensing and attribution drift. About 20 OGA picks under CC-BY/OGA-BY plus Openverse images are credited by hand in CREDITS.md and in a title-screen line (screens.ts:131-132) with no machine-readable mapping from file to source. Swapping a sprite can leave the credits wrong.
- Style coherence is getting worse. Flayer, carcass, hook and all the older bosses are still code-drawn (vault Grodius.md:82) while the regular enemies are repainted bitmap strips. Without an art bible, every new pass widens that gap.
- Hazard false positives. Ambient particles and the acid/snot gore palettes (biomes.ts:44, 307) share the bullet signature. Colour-blind players (lime against teal/sulfur in the acid and Sinus biomes) have no alternative.
- Mobile GPU fill rate has not been measured. perf-probe uses Windows ANGLE flags (perf-probe.mjs:14) and CPU throttling only. The cost of the ctx.filter hit flash (enemies.ts:1047) and of full-screen washes at DK 4 on real Android WebView is unknown.
- Payload: music is 38 MB of the roughly 45 MB of shipped assets (du on src/music). Image optimisation such as WebP backdrops saves well under 1 MB, so it should not be prioritised over the audio decision.
- Changing render order or the RNG calls will shift the harness baselines. The baseline needs a deliberate reset, coordinated with whoever owns tools/harness.mjs, so a real regression is not hidden inside it.

### Decisions for Matt
- How gross should the dial go, and who gets to turn it? Is the single GROSS/FX tier only an internal art and performance knob, or a player-facing option (for example TAME / GROSS / ULTIMATE) alongside HELL FLASHES?
- With HELL FLASHES OFF, should the white and red washes and the glitch tearing be removed entirely, or capped (for example at 0.2 alpha)? And should the flash warning become a first-launch gate screen that offers the setting right there?
- Visual direction for the remaining code-drawn enemies and bosses (Flayer, carcass, hook, older bosses): move them to repainted bitmap strips like the 2026-10-28 pass, or keep the hand-drawn vector look as a deliberate contrast?
- Enemy-fire colour: is lime locked as the permanent hazard colour, and do you want a colour-blind alternative bullet palette (for example magenta/white) as an option?
- Terminology: choose one name per system. MUTATION for run perk cards, and then separate words for kill-driven growth, permanent Infirmary upgrades and socketed contraptions. Currently 'GRAFT' covers several of these.
- Is it acceptable to commit an assets-raw manifest (source URLs, licenses, hashes) to the public repo, or should raw-source provenance stay private?
- Payload: re-encode the music at a lower bitrate, or lazy-load the tracks for Descent 2 and later? This affects APK size far more than any image work.

#### technical-artist

Grodius draws everything with Canvas2D on a 480x270 low-res buffer (lo, core.ts:7-24). present() scales that buffer up to a display canvas at DK x 2-4 (eyes.ts:488-505, input.ts:193-221), and a queue of high-resolution 'real' eyeballs (EYEQ) is drawn straight onto the display canvas. Most of the art is still code-drawn vector work: render/enemies.ts is 1219 lines, shipparts.ts 1167, and the five gross/* boss modules are about 244 KB. Bitmap art goes on top of that. There are 32 strip sprites in src/sprites, named name@frames.png and loaded through an eager import.meta.glob (sprites.ts:9-17), with code-drawn fallbacks while they load. There are 16 photos and textures in src/img: three JWST/Hubble skies at 960x254, six 128 px RODA tissue tiles used as repeat patterns (textures.ts:24-29), and X-rays. Sprite FX are in src/fx/fxsprites.ts: per-biome hit sparks and Tyrian explosions in preallocated rings, with an additive glow pass. Smoke and rings are in gunfx.ts. Expensive bakes (photo irises, boss art, the Gravity Drive) are built in idle slices after boot (main.ts:71-75, eyes.ts:231-300). All raw inputs live in the git-ignored assets-raw/, which does not exist on this laptop clone. Image assets are small: sprites total about 455 KB and src/img about 1.2 MB. Music dominates the shipped payload at 38 MB of OGG at about 112 kbps, out of about 45 MB of assets in src.

## Audio

Grodius has a good sample palette and a sensible pipeline: shuffle bags on their own RNG, synth fallbacks, gore layered by kill size, a gun that gets heavier with power tier, per-biome music colouring, and seeded generators. Its weaknesses are mix architecture and the contract between sound triggers and the engine. Everything (SFX, voice, music, reverb return and an always-on 41-82 Hz drone) sums into one gain of .55, then one -16 dB / 5:1 compressor on default soft-knee settings (src/audio.ts:22-28, 47-86; src/music.ts:86). The music bus, normalised to -16 LUFS, runs through that same compressor, so gore can audibly pump the soundtrack. The 14-voice cap has no priority and returns true when it drops a sound (audio.ts:127), so voice lines, sirens and the death samples can disappear in exactly the busiest moments, and the fallbacks written for them never run. Voice delivery is half-finished. Only hype10 and babe20 have recordings, and both include the same shamalama-matty-1.ogg take (src/sfx/hype10, src/sfx/babe20). Milestones 35/50/75/100 fall to robotic TTS that skips music ducking (audio.ts:707-728). A shared 20 s cooldown (src/fx/update.ts:219, 235-237) usually swallows the 20-chain surfer, because the 10-chain line starts that cooldown. Lines are scheduled with setTimeout, not game time (fx/update.ts:280-286, 299-301). Player controls are two toggles; the mute is applied instantly with no ramp and is not saved by setMute (audio.ts:730-733, menus.ts:38-49, step.ts:73). There is no audio spec, no event table, and five incompatible snd() wrappers in render/gross/*. I checked the sound-designer's report against the code and it is broadly accurate. I rejected or narrowed three claims: unbounded synth nodes, a silent death, and 'little stinker' being wrong. I also add a licensing risk the specialist missed: the APK credits CC BY music without the licence or the individual Freesound CC BY artists. My order is: compliance first, then the mix/priority fix, which unblocks everything else, then the voice-line decision, then the event API and spec. The new design/ folder has no audio docs, and its framework templates assume an engine (Godot/Unity/Unreal) that isn't used here; any audio spec must describe the Web Audio graph that actually exists.

### Risks
- Licensing: the CC BY 4.0 music (dancramp Void, Doctor_Dreamchip, ztitchez space-wind) ships in the Pages build and the APK without the licence named, and without the individual Freesound creators in game (src/render/screens.ts:132). The public repo also contradicts itself (README.md:110 'CC0 only' versus CREDITS.md:3-4).
- Retuning the master compressor or splitting buses changes the loudness of every sound at once. Without a reference capture, the result depends on whoever listens. Capture before/after on a dense scene (Maw, Gravity Drive) and on Android speakers, not just headphones.
- @ts-nocheck on src/audio.ts:1 and src/music.ts:1 means a bus or priority refactor has no type safety, and harness.mjs does not cover audio. Regressions such as a silent category or a broken fallback will not be caught unless a sample()-call logging probe is added (AU.lastCat is already exposed as a test hook).
- Music loop seams: the dancramp tracks have a baked 2 s fade, then HTMLAudioElement loop=true restarts them (CREDITS.md:20-21, src/music.ts:88-90). On long stages the music dips and restarts, and the Android WebView media-element loop gap makes it worse. A fix means re-exporting from assets-raw/ (only on Matty's machine), which other contributors cannot reproduce.
- APK size: src/music is 38 MB of Vorbis q3 against 3.8 MB of SFX. Adding more tracks or voice takes without a re-encode policy (Opus) grows the download quickly.
- TTS fallback output varies a lot by platform and voice (speechSynthesis voice regex at audio.ts:719; native TTS on Android). The game's voice identity is not under our control while TTS ships as a default path.

#### sound-designer

Grodius uses plain Web Audio with no audio middleware. src/audio.ts (738 lines, @ts-nocheck) builds one AudioContext. The chain is a master gain (.55) into a DynamicsCompressor (-16 dB threshold, 5:1) into the output, with a generated stereo-noise convolver reverb as the single send (audio.ts:22-46). There is also a synth "drone bed" that is always on: detuned saws at 41-82 Hz plus low-passed noise (audio.ts:47-86), and fx/update.ts:314 changes its filter as the run gets deeper. Every file under src/sfx/<category>/ is bundled with import.meta.glob and decoded on first input (audio.ts:94-107). There are 29 categories (3.8 MB): procedural gen-* clips from tools/gen_sfx.py, gen_gross_sfx.py, gen_drive_sfx.py and gen_bossgross_sfx.py, Kenney CC0 impacts, Openverse/Freesound cuts (ov-*), and Matty's own recordings (farts, voice lines). sample() plays from a per-category shuffle bag, using its own audio RNG with pitch and volume jitter, and a global cap of 14 voices (audio.ts:108-142). Everything else is synthesized live through the `sfx` table (shots by power tier, laser, missile, hit, gore fallback, roar, siren, heart, purge...) built on tone()/nz() (audio.ts:168-725). Voice lines in the hype10, babe20, bossreward, corpsevoice and startgame categories duck the music and drive the CRT "comms window" faces in render/hype.ts. Chain milestones without a recording fall back to speechSynthesis, or to Capacitor native TTS on Android (audio.ts:707-728, platform.ts:30-37).

Music (src/music.ts) uses two HTMLAudioElement decks fed through MediaElementSource into a bus. The bus runs through a biquad filter, an optional LFO and a tanh waveshaper, then into the same master. It crossfades over 1.4 s, drops to .35 under pause and to .3 under voice lines (music.ts:48, 154). TRACKS maps slots to 19 Ogg files (38 MB): dancramp's "Void" CC BY plus Openverse CC0/CC BY tracks, with lists that rotate per descent. BIOME_MIX gives each biome a track and a filter colour, plus "+" remix filters for repeat visits and a UFO wah (music.ts:30-47). Players get exactly two controls: SOUND (a master mute) and MUSIC (on/off) (menus.ts:38-49). There is no audio spec, event list or mix document anywhere in design/ or docs/. The only documentation is README.md:104-112, CREDITS.md and CHANGELOG notes.

### Risks
- Agent-driven copy drift: the CCGS framework will now let many agents write text, but there is no strings table and no registry. A retired name (Crucifer, crosses) or the wrong register will come back unless entities.yaml and the voice doc land first.
- Renaming internal ids (kind 'cross', 'crux', set 'crucify') touches gameplay code that tools/harness.mjs and the probes depend on. Narrative should rename only player-facing strings and comments, and leave enemy kind keys alone unless engineering signs off with a harness run.
- If the gross-out pass is over-applied it could flatten the horror straight-man that makes the gross beats land. If it is under-applied, the text keeps contradicting the 'ultimate gross-out' pitch in Grodius.md:9.
- Profanity in names that show on the title, fork and HUD (THE SQUIDFUCKED SAUCER, BLOATED BITCH), with a content warning that covers only gore and flashing images (src/render/screens.ts:130), is a store-listing and age-rating risk if the game ever leaves sideload/Pages.
- Unwrapped 10px gothic flavour text in 142px perk cards (src/render/hud.ts:575,642) can clip if new or longer lines are written. Every copy change needs a screenshot check with the *-shots.mjs rigs.

Then fix the photosensitivity issues, which carry real player-safety and store risk on Android. Broader accessibility (remapping, assists, auto-fire) is valuable but comes after those.

Note: the .claude/production/design framework folders exist but are unconfigured (design/ holds only CLAUDE.md and registry; production/ only session-state). There is no test-evidence convention yet. ### Risks
- Tune branches are merged in parallel by agents with no gate. The next coefficient change (or a merge conflict like 4b0d6ab) ships to the 'latest' APK and Pages immediately (build.yml:3-6).
- Any tuning conclusion drawn from 'harness identical' is unverifiable right now. The documented baseline has moved three times without a committed artifact, so a real desync cannot be told apart from an expected baseline shift.
- Canvas-hash determinism will stay broken until render code stops calling gameplay R/rr/ri in branches that depend on sprite-load and idle-callback timing (src/render/hell.ts imports R/ri/rr at :3; src/sprites.ts:7-9 states the rule; requestIdleCallback in src/main.ts and src/render/eyes.ts). Asserting on hashes too early will produce flaky CI and erode trust in the gate.
- Coverage gap: the harness never leaves Open Orbit in Descent 1 and ends on the game-over screen (docs/pacing.md:310). Elites, depth scaling, remixes, bosses, the Infirmary and the POWER meter mode, where all of the October tuning landed, have no regression coverage at all.
- d0115f4 lets every() compress the fire interval down to the 18-frame floor at pow 2.0 (n*(1-.8)=0.2n), and formation() adds up to 6 bodies per wave. Pressure past the cap is now bounded only by the floor and the eshots>260 cap. A difficulty spike or a frame-time regression on low-end Android is plausible and unmeasured.
- Photosensitivity: flashes default ON, and the OFF setting leaves screen-wide glitch, red flash and flicker running. That exposure is real for a public Pages build and a sideloaded APK, and grows if the game goes to a store.
- Adding Playwright and chromium to CI adds about 1-2 minutes and a possible flake source. The harness's setTimeout no-op (harness.mjs:12) also hides timer-driven logic (warmUp, gate clear), so a green harness does not prove the real boot path works.

### Decisions for Matt
- Should HELL FLASHES default to OFF, or stay ON behind a first-launch photosensitivity prompt at the 'press any key' gate? And should the OFF setting also kill the glitch tear, red flash and darkness flicker, or should these become separate toggles?
- Is it acceptable for CI to block deploys (verify job as `needs:` for the android and web jobs), and to move tuning work onto PRs instead of direct pushes to main? Today d0115f4 and 48eb7cb were committed straight to main.
- Is the post-tune-pressure behavior (Descent 1 now responds to G.pow with stronger coefficients) intended to change Descent 1? If so, is the new harness `st` the accepted baseline? If not, should Descent 1 stay pinned to the old rank curve?
- Should the canvas hashes become a hard gate? That requires a cleanup moving render-side R/rr/ri to srnd(). The alternative is to accept `st`-only determinism and treat hashes as advisory permanently.
- How much accessibility scope beyond safety fixes do you want before release: control remapping, an auto-fire toggle for keyboard and gamepad, a screen shake slider, assist options (game speed, extra lives), a gore-intensity dial? And should assisted runs be marked on high scores?
- Should the Claude Code Game Studios framework's test-evidence and Definition-of-Done gates apply to this project (qa.level / testing.strict in project.yaml)? Or should QA keep a lightweight Node-based convention (tools/ plus tests/unit) suited to a solo, engine-less project?

### Specialist claims the lead rejected
- qa-tester, 'Render code consumes the gameplay RNG ... making canvas hashes nondeterministic' is overstated as a causal chain. A render-side R() call that runs on every frame is deterministic under the seeded stream. It shifts the stream but does not cause run-to-run variance, and docs/pacing.md:199,310 report `st` as identical across runs. The variance comes specifically from R() calls in branches that depend on sprite-load timing or requestIdleCallback timing (sprite fallbacks, src/render/eyes.ts iris queue). The fix is still right, but priority should go to the timing-dependent callers, not a blanket count of 17 calls in hell.ts. Most of hell.ts's calls run only while G.glitch or G.hell is active, which is itself sim-driven.
- qa-tester, 'CHANGELOG.md:13 still claims Descent 1 is unchanged': the line is CHANGELOG.md:9, not :13. The sentence describes the earlier depth-HP change, for which it was true when written. It is stale now because of d0115f4, but it was not wrong at the time.
- accessibility-specialist, 'a large brightness swing at up to 30Hz, well beyond the WCAG 2.3.1 limit of 3 flashes per second' is overstated for a single hellFlash. G.hell lasts only 3-5 frames (fx/update.ts:306, ri(3,5)), so per-frame blend re-rolls produce at most about 2 extra transitions inside one event of roughly 80ms, not sustained 30Hz strobing. The real 2.3.1 risk is clusters of events: the boss death sequence (boss/update.ts:96-99, up to about 8 events in 220 frames) and stacked white, red and glitch flashes. The issue keeps its high severity, but the reason is clusters and the default being ON, not a 30Hz strobe.
- accessibility-specialist, 'There is a haptics on/off flag, but no setter or menu entry ever calls it' is technically imprecise. The setter exists (src/platform.ts:27 setBuzzOff, not :34 as cited). What is missing is any caller or menu item. That is a minor finding and does not belong among QA priorities.
- qa-tester, '52 of the 54 TypeScript files start with @ts-nocheck' is confirmed (only src/state.ts and src/platform.ts lack it). But the recommendation to put typecheck in CI now would add little gate value until pure modules are opted back in. I deprioritised it below the harness and probe gates rather than rejecting it.

#### accessibility-specialist

Grodius has one accessibility setting, HELL FLASHES ON/OFF (src/save.ts:6, toggled from the title menu at src/menus.ts:31-37 and saved to localStorage). The hub doc treats it as something that must keep working (Grodius.md:113). When the setting is on, it allows the full-screen "hell face" strobe (src/fx/update.ts:303-309, drawn at src/render/hell.ts:72-79) and the full-screen tints that boss ruptures add (src/render/gross/maw.ts:1065, ufo.ts:613, drive.ts:692). When it is off, the white screen flash is dimmed to 40% (src/render/hell.ts:85). The flashes are on by default. Three other effects ignore the setting entirely: the scanline-tear "glitch" (hell.ts:66-71), screen shake (step.ts:93-96) and the darkness flicker in corridor biomes (fx/update.ts:317, hud.ts:479). The title screen shows "EXTREME GORE. FLASHING IMAGES." in very dark red (src/render/screens.ts:130). The only other settings are POWER mode (pods or meter), SOUND and MUSIC.

Input is hardcoded with no rebinding. The keyboard is fixed to Arrows/WASD, Z/Space/J and X/Shift/K (src/input.ts:61-69). The gamepad is assumed to use the standard mapping and only the first connected pad is read (input.ts:71-95). Touch play uses relative drag with auto-fire. There is a haptics on/off flag, but no setter or menu entry ever calls it. There is no difficulty or assist option. Lives are 2 plus the Infirmary hull upgrade (flow.ts:23), and the pressure "rank" rises automatically with the player's power (step.ts:27, depth.ts). The display is a 480x270 canvas scaled up, so all text is drawn in-canvas at 7-10px game pixels (render/util.ts:8). Screen readers and the OS text-size setting have no effect on it, and nothing in the game reads OS accessibility preferences. The CSS vignette is the one exception: it checks prefers-reduced-motion (src/style.css:14).

### Risks
- Moving to stable signing forces one final uninstall on the Fold, so current saves are lost unless an export or backup exists first. src/main.ts:77-81 has an unused import path that skips migration, so it is not a safe backup route as written.
- The baseline can be frozen at the wrong value. CHANGELOG.md:26 (3028/2168) and docs/pacing.md:199 (1332/3509) disagree, and both predate 48eb7cb. Freezing either one makes the new gate fail on day one or bless stale behaviour.
- Harness determinism covers only `st`. Gating on frame hashes would give flaky red builds (docs/pacing.md:310).
- The CCGS framework may add its own workflows. Renaming or replacing build.yml resets GITHUB_RUN_NUMBER and so versionCode (build.gradle:11), and the next APK will not install over the old one.
- The shipped APK is debuggable with webContentsDebuggingEnabled: true (capacitor.config.ts:11, build.yml:51). Acceptable for personal sideload, but it must not be the artifact handed to anyone else.
- Public repo with no LICENSE (none at the repo root, no license field in package.json). The live Pages build and APK can be redistributed with no stated terms. CREDITS.md:33 attributes all *-matty-* voice files to Matty, but commit 0072992 adds a third party's recording.
- build.yml has `permissions: contents: write` at workflow level, so npm ci and gradle steps also run with write access (build.yml:8-9).

#### live-ops-designer

Grodius is a free, offline, single-player game with no backend. It sideloads as an APK from a rolling `latest` GitHub release and also runs as a GitHub Pages web build. Both are rebuilt on every push to main (.github/workflows/build.yml:66-75). There is no store listing, server, account, analytics, telemetry or network call in src/. The only network-adjacent code is Capacitor glue for haptics and TTS (src/platform.ts). So the usual live-ops surfaces (battle passes, live events, server-side tuning, engagement analytics) don't apply. What does apply is (a) content cadence: the "update" is a push to main, and the CHANGELOG shows a very high cadence (two big content drops in three days, CHANGELOG.md:5-60), and (b) offline retention: the loops that bring a single player back to a run-based game.

Today's retention layer is small. All of it is a single localStorage blob `grodius.v1` (src/save.ts:21,62-72) holding biomass, Infirmary levels, contraptions, one all-time high score and a best grade per stage label (src/stages.ts:67-70). The run loop is endless descents (src/flow.ts:214-219 → openShop('loop')). From Descent 2, hand-made section remixes are picked by gameplay RNG (src/enemies/scripts.ts:5-38), and repeat biome visits are remixed (src/biomes.ts:37-72,181,238). Gameplay RNG is plain Math.random (src/core.ts:33). The harness can only make runs deterministic by monkey-patching it (tools/harness.mjs:8), but audio and sprites are already strictly isolated from gameplay RNG (src/audio.ts:97-99, src/sprites.ts:7, src/fx/fxsprites.ts:5). Combined with data-driven content tables (BIOMES, script variants with `min` descent gates, the contraption table T), that makes the codebase unusually ready for offline "live-ops-lite": seeded daily or weekly runs, rotating modifiers and date-gated content, none of which needs a server.

## Localization

Grodius has no localization layer and nothing in the docs sets one up. README, CHANGELOG, docs/pacing.md and the vault never mention languages, locales or translation. The game ships as a sideloaded APK plus GitHub Pages (vault Mirrors/README.md:16), so there is no store or market forcing translation. All player-facing text is hardcoded English and drawn straight onto a 480x270 canvas through one helper, txt() at src/render/util.ts:8. A rough count finds 300+ uppercase string literals in 22 files: screens.ts 51, hud.ts 39, perks.ts 27, menus.ts 27, biomes.ts 26, mutations.ts 25, player.ts 21 and others. On top of that are lowercase descriptions and flavour lines in perks.ts:15-40 and player.ts:127-133, which are uppercased when drawn (hud.ts:202, 636). Text is built by English-only concatenation, word order and plurals: perks.ts:18 `enem${l > 1 ? 'ies' : 'y'}`, hud.ts:101 'STAGE ' + label + ' ' + name, hud.ts:656 perkName(k).split(' ').pop(), and Roman-numeral ranks from ROM[]. Layout uses fixed pixel columns: screens.ts:35 x+206, screens.ts:331-345 a column at x=136, hud.ts:503 x0+128. wrapTxt splits on spaces and silently drops anything past 4 lines (screens.ts:197-210). The two bundled fonts (@fontsource Silkscreen 400 and Grenze Gotisch 600/900, imported at main.ts:3-5) cover only Latin, Latin-ext and Vietnamese. There is no Cyrillic, CJK, Arabic or Devanagari. A 7-8px pixel font and a blackletter display face could not carry CJK anyway. Voice lines are Matt's own English recordings (src/sfx/hype10, babe20, corpsevoice) with TTS fallback. The hype lines HYPE[35/50/75/100] (fx/update.ts:248-253) have no recorded clips, so TTS is what actually plays for them, and the browser TTS path never sets a language. My view: adding i18n now would be premature. What is worth doing is (a) getting a scope decision from Matt, (b) fixing the one real locale bug that affects English players today (TTS voice language), and (c) a few cheap guardrails (a transcript per voice clip, a text-fit check, and no new English-grammar concatenation) so a later translation pass costs a little rather than a rewrite. One good pattern to keep: the comms-window caption shrinks to fit (render/hype.ts:404-406, z = w > 118 ? ... : 11).

### Risks
- Silent text loss: wrapTxt drops lines after the 4th with no ellipsis (src/render/screens.ts:209). A longer English perk or contraption description is cut off without anyone noticing, and no test or harness checks text width.
- Game logic keyed on display strings: hud.ts:516 picks a colour from t.best.startsWith('NEW') / startsWith('FIRST'), and hype.ts:281-287 maps voice faces from English clip file names. Renaming or translating these strings breaks behaviour, not just text.
- TTS fallback is the only audio path for 4 of the 6 hype milestones (35/50/75/100) and plays in the system's default language on non-English browsers (src/audio.ts:721). Players outside English locales already hear garbled lines.
- The font loader preloads only the Latin subset ('8px Silkscreen', main.ts:138). Latin-ext glyphs (any accented name or credit) load lazily, so the first canvas frame that uses one draws in the Courier New/monospace fallback.
- If translation is ever attempted, CJK at 7-8px on a pixelated 480x270 canvas is not readable with system fallback fonts. It would need a bitmap font and probably larger text sizes, which changes HUD layout (hud.ts fixed coordinates throughout).
- Content-rating exposure if Grodius ever goes to a store: extreme gore, a 'stillborn' or fetus-digestion biome (Mirrors/CHANGELOG.md:109) and slur-based boss names can trigger regional rating and age-gate issues (Germany/USK, and Google Play's IARC questionnaire). That is a localization and compliance cost, not just a translation one.
- The framework's /localize skill and production/localization outputs (docs/skill-flow-diagrams.md:134) assume a string-table engine workflow. Running it as-is against this codebase would produce wrong artifacts.

### Decisions for Matt
- Is Grodius English-only for the foreseeable future? If not, which languages, and only Latin-script ones (which rules out Cyrillic and CJK given the pixel fonts)?
- Will Grodius ever go to a store (Google Play, itch, Steam), or stay a sideload plus GitHub Pages project? This decides whether rating and regional content review is needed at all.
- Are boss and enemy names like 'BLOATED BITCH', 'UNCRUCIFIED' and 'STILLBORN' fixed creative choices, or open to regional variants if the game is ever translated or rated?
- Should every voice line have an on-screen caption with the exact words (for accessibility and future translation), or should some clips stay caption-less on purpose for comedic effect?
- If you record more voice lines, will you keep recording only in English with your own voice? Then TTS fallback should be English-only on purpose, rather than following the device language.

### Specialist claims the lead rejected
- Claim checked: 'String.prototype.toUpperCase() on perk descriptions (hud.ts:202, 636, 639) breaks on Turkish or German devices.' Rejected: toUpperCase() is locale-independent; only toLocaleUpperCase() follows the device locale. These calls are safe as written. ß becomes SS, which is correct.
- Claim checked: 'toLocaleString('en-US') at hud.ts:514 is a locale bug.' Rejected for current scope: for an English-only game, pinning en-US is correct and gives stable score formatting. It matters only once other locales exist.
- Claim checked: 'There are no fonts bundled, so text falls back to system fonts.' Rejected: Silkscreen and Grenze Gotisch are bundled via @fontsource imports (main.ts:3-5) and preloaded before start (main.ts:138). The gap is script coverage, not missing fonts.
- Claim checked: 'The game needs a full i18n system (locale JSON per feature, ICU MessageFormat, fallback chains) now, as the role standard prescribes.' Rejected as premature: no languages are in scope, distribution is sideload-only, and the cost would fall on 300+ literals across 22 files. The cheap guardrails above keep that option open.
